## 🇬🇧 English

### 🐛 Fixes

- **A recent season is no longer rejected because the year given by the metadata is wrong.**
- **A series like Pokémon can now get the theme of its latest season instead of an older one.**
- **To get it for a series that already has themes, ask for an update of the themes.**

### 🔧 Internal: nothing changes on screen

- **New tests cover the choice of the seasons to look up, with the real data of a Pokémon series.**

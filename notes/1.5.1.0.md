## 🇬🇧 English

### ✨ Improvement

- **OpEdSync now has its own entry in the dashboard menu, next to the other plugins.**
- **The settings page opens at once: the figures fill in a moment later instead of holding the whole page.**
- **A clearer layout: the four figures line up on large screens, libraries fit on two or three columns with their number of items, and each section has its icon.**
- **Clearer wording throughout the page, with the same words as Jellyfin for its theme songs setting.**

### 🐛 Fixes

- **"Next run" now shows the real time of a daily or weekly schedule instead of "manual".**
- **The last run reads "+22 themes · no failures" instead of a bare number and "0 failed".**
